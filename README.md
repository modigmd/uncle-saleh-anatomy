# Uncle Saleh Anatomy

[Open the interactive viewer](https://modigmd.github.io/uncle-saleh-anatomy/) · [Watch and download the hand films](https://modigmd.github.io/uncle-saleh-anatomy/videos.html)

The viewer opens on the complete standing figure in the black theme, with original colours and 33% shell reveal. Inspection and separation controls come before the motion showcases. It includes 344 inspectable parts: 125 uploaded STL head components and 219 retained CAD components, with the original face and body. Drag through 360°, or choose Move for vertical positioning and use its adjacent reset. Hands and arms inspect one side at a time; Both remains available for showcases. The middle chest frame stays centred as the shoulders separate. Shell reveal can be changed during playback. Hands and head run for 20 seconds, arms for 18, and shoulders for 20. The shorter sequences preserve every original movement. The head's internal jaw and lower teeth open with the face; eyelid caps and a local face blink close the eyes during head tilts. Added shoulder Lift, Sweep and Roll gestures carry the attached arms, before the retained 67 finale. The standalone HTML opens offline and uses lossless compressed payloads, deferred SVG fallback, focused GPU drawing and reusable face-morph buffers to reduce file size and rendering work while retaining mesh detail.

Six hand films use H.264 at 1920×1080 and 60 fps, with soft plastic clicks following flexes and releases. Right, left and both hands run for 25 seconds. Three right-hand mechanism films run for 40 seconds, combining the complete hand sequence with one slow 360° turn: black/graphite, black/source colours and white/source colours. Video metadata and source provenance are recorded in videos/export-manifest.json.

Motion and sound are authored for presentation; they do not establish servo commands, operating limits or physical linkage behaviour.

Built using the [Anatomy skill](https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy), source CAD meshes, and [Three.js](https://threejs.org/).
