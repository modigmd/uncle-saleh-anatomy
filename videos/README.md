# Uncle Saleh hand videos

Six H.264 MP4 videos at 1920 × 1080 and 60 fps, with JPEG posters and verification contact sheets.

All six videos include quiet stereo plastic clicks synchronized with finger, thumb and wrist movement. Releases sound softer, with slight stereo positioning. There is no music, speech or continuous background sound. The clicks are synthetic presentation accents derived from the approved motion timeline.

The 360° videos turn at a constant 9° per second while playing the approved 25-second hand sequence retimed to 40 seconds. A fixed camera covers the full motion and rotation. The mechanisms use complete source CAD surfaces, including rear faces; no generated replacement shapes are used.

This is an authored source-part pose study. Operating linkages, gear coupling and servo limits are not verified.

Audio uses 48 kHz stereo AAC with a requested 192 kbps encoder setting. Single-hand and orbit peaks stay at −17 dBTP; both hands peak at −15.5 dBTP. The final video uses high-quality lossy x264 CRF16 / medium compression. Frame presentation times are unchanged; AAC audio packets and the decoded waveform are preserved exactly.

`export-manifest.json` records source hashes, codecs, frame counts, file hashes and sizes. `video-verification.json` records full decoding, camera bounds, pixel loop closure and visual inspection. `audio-verification.json` records measured levels, timing alignment, silent loop boundaries and unchanged final AAC audio. `compression-verification.json` records savings, full decoding, frame timing and full-frame/foreground/caption SSIM.
